02 / Services

Resolution, dialogue, progress.

Structured, practical support for disputes, negotiations and difficult conversations.

What we do

Support designed for the matter in front of you.

Disputes can disrupt relationships, businesses and important decisions. We help clients understand the issues, evaluate available options and engage constructively with a focus on workable outcomes.

01Business disputes

Dispute Resolution for Companies

Business disputes can affect operations, commercial relationships, reputation and long-term strategy. Arende Oriri & Associates Advocates assists companies and organisations in addressing disputes through structured and solution-focused processes, with an emphasis on reducing unnecessary disruption and preserving valuable relationships where possible.

Companies · Commercial relationships · Workplace & organisational disputes
02Mediation

Mediation Services

Mediation gives parties an opportunity to discuss a dispute in a structured and confidential environment. We facilitate constructive dialogue, clarify areas of agreement and disagreement, and support parties as they explore possible solutions.

Preparation · Structured dialogue · Workable agreement
03Facilitation

Meeting Facilitation Services

Important meetings can become difficult when participants hold competing interests or discussions involve sensitive issues. We help structure discussions, manage participation, clarify issues and keep meetings focused, balanced and productive.

Companies · Organisations · Management teams · Stakeholders
04Negotiation

Negotiation Consulting

Effective negotiation requires preparation, clarity and a proper understanding of interests and risks. We help clients identify priorities, assess options, anticipate areas of disagreement and approach negotiations with greater confidence.

Strategy · Preparation · Negotiation support
05Individuals

Dispute Resolution for Individuals

Personal disputes can be stressful and difficult to navigate without clear guidance. We assist individuals in understanding the issues involved, exploring appropriate resolution options and making informed decisions according to the circumstances of each matter.

Assessment · Options · Constructive resolution
06ADR

Alternative Dispute Resolution

Alternative Dispute Resolution provides ways of addressing disputes outside traditional adversarial proceedings. We assist with employment and labour issues, children and family-related disputes, land matters and other situations where structured negotiation, mediation or facilitated discussion may be appropriate.

Employment & labour · Children & family-related matters · Land matters
