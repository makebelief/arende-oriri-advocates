About Arende Oriri & Associates Advocates

Human rights advocacy with a public-interest focus.

A Nairobi-based boutique law firm focused on human rights litigation, public interest litigation and general legal services.

Who we are

Rights-focused counsel.
Strategic litigation.

Arende Oriri & Associates Advocates is based in Nairobi, Kenya. Its public profile identifies human rights litigation and public interest litigation as core areas, together with general legal services and work across a broad range of rights-related issues.

01 / Core focus

Human rights & public-interest litigation.

The firm’s documented focus is human rights litigation and public interest litigation, including strategic matters with wider social or institutional significance.

02 / Location

Nairobi, Kenya.

The practice is based in Nairobi and its public network profile identifies its presence in Kenya.

03 / Rights areas

A broad rights-based practice.

Publicly identified focus areas include disability rights, environmental justice, governance and accountability, housing rights, labour and employment, refugees and migrants, right to information and women’s rights.

04 / General services

General legal services.

Alongside its public-interest work, the firm’s public profile states that it offers general legal services.

Areas of rights work

A practice shaped by rights and access to justice.

The firm’s published areas of focus span multiple human-rights and public-interest fields.

01

Rights, equality, inclusion and access for persons with disabilities are identified areas of focus.

02

The practice’s public profile includes environmental justice, while Kevin Arende Oriri’s public work also engages food sovereignty and environmental accountability.

How we work

From first consultation to legal strategy.

01 / Initial consultation

We begin by understanding the facts, the rights engaged and the client’s immediate objectives.

02 / Assessment

We assess the legal framework, evidence, procedure and any wider public-interest implications.

03 / Engagement

The next step may involve advice, correspondence, strategic litigation, constitutional proceedings or other appropriate legal action.

04 / Resolution & next steps

We keep the client informed on the status, implications and next steps as the matter develops.
