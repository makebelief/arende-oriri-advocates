About Nero & Partners

Constructive thinking for complex moments.

A solutions-oriented practice helping individuals, businesses and organisations approach disputes with clarity, structure and a focus on workable outcomes.

Who we are

Focused counsel.
Practical resolution.

Nero & Partners assists individuals, companies and organisations in navigating disagreements through structured processes that encourage constructive dialogue, informed decision-making and practical resolution. Our approach is professional, confidential and responsive to the circumstances of each matter.

01 / Mission

Help people address conflict constructively.

Our mission is to provide accessible, professional and practical dispute-resolution services that help individuals and organisations move forward with confidence.

02 / Vision

A trusted practice for practical resolution.

Our vision is to be recognised for professionalism, integrity, constructive engagement and practical solutions.

03 / Approach

Understand first. Then move with purpose.

We focus on understanding the issues, reducing unnecessary conflict and identifying realistic paths forward.

04 / Areas served

Nakuru & Nairobi.

Based at Gatehouse, 4th Floor, Mburu Gichua Road, Nakuru, with services available across Nakuru and Nairobi.

Our values

Standards that guide every engagement.

Our work is guided by professionalism, confidentiality, integrity, practicality, respect and clarity.

01

How we work

A clear process from first conversation.

01 / Initial consultation

We begin by understanding the nature of the dispute, the parties involved and the client’s objectives.

02 / Assessment

We consider the issues and identify dispute-resolution approaches that may be appropriate.

03 / Engagement

This may involve mediation, negotiation support, facilitated meetings or another structured process.

04 / Resolution & next steps

We help the client understand the outcome and any further steps that may be required.
