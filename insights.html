Topics we explore

Practical thinking for common dispute questions.

These topics are designed to help individuals and organisations understand dispute-resolution processes before making important decisions.

Practice note · 01

Mediation or litigation: understanding the difference

Mediation is a structured process aimed at helping parties explore agreement, while litigation places the dispute before a court for determination. The appropriate route depends on the nature of the matter, the parties’ objectives and the circumstances involved.

Negotiation · 02

Five things to consider before entering a negotiation

Effective preparation includes identifying priorities, understanding alternatives, anticipating likely areas of disagreement, deciding what information matters and being clear about the outcome you are seeking.

Business · 03

Preventing commercial disputes from escalating

Early communication, clear documentation and structured discussion can help organisations identify problems before positions harden and preserve useful commercial relationships where possible.

Frequently asked

Good questions are a starting point.

These general answers are designed to make the first conversation easier. The appropriate approach always depends on the circumstances of the particular matter.

01

What types of disputes does Arende Oriri & Associates Advocates assist with?

We assist individuals, companies and organisations with dispute-resolution processes involving commercial disagreements, employment and labour matters, land-related disputes, children and family-related matters, negotiations and facilitated discussions.

02

What is mediation?

Mediation is a structured process in which parties discuss a dispute with the assistance of a neutral facilitator and explore whether a mutually acceptable resolution can be reached.

03

Is mediation confidential?

Mediation is generally conducted on a confidential basis. The specific confidentiality arrangements applicable to an engagement should be discussed before the process begins.

04

Do I have to go to court before considering mediation?

Not necessarily. Depending on the circumstances, parties may consider mediation or another dispute-resolution process before, during or alongside formal proceedings.

05

Can businesses use your services?

Yes. Arende Oriri & Associates Advocates provides dispute-resolution and negotiation support to companies and organisations as well as individuals.

06

Do you provide negotiation assistance?

Yes. We assist with preparation, strategy and support for negotiations involving commercial, workplace and other disputes.

07

How do I get started?

Contact us by telephone, WhatsApp, email or through the website enquiry form and provide a brief description of the matter.

08

Does contacting the practice mean my matter has been accepted?

No. An initial enquiry helps us understand the nature of the matter. A professional engagement begins only after appropriate terms have been agreed.
