About the practice

What the public record supports.

The answers below distinguish verified public information from general information about engaging a law firm.

01

What is the firm’s core focus?

The firm publicly describes itself as a boutique law firm in Nairobi specialising in human rights litigation and public interest litigation, while also offering general legal services.

02

Who is Kevin Arende Oriri?

Kevin Arende Oriri is a human rights lawyer and Advocate of the High Court of Kenya. His public professional profile identifies strategic human rights litigation as a specialisation.

03

Where is the practice based?

The firm’s public profile identifies Nairobi, Kenya as its location and Kenya as its area of presence.

04

What is public-interest litigation?

Public-interest litigation concerns legal questions whose effects may extend beyond the immediate parties, including constitutional rights, institutional accountability or systemic issues.

05

Does the firm work on environmental justice?

Yes. Environmental justice is one of the focus areas publicly identified for the firm, and Kevin Arende Oriri’s public work includes food-sovereignty advocacy.

06

Does the firm work on disability rights?

Yes. Disability rights are specifically listed among the firm’s public areas of focus, and Kevin’s profile records pro bono work with the Kenya Union of the Blind.

07

How do I get started?

Call or WhatsApp +254 722 948 247, or use the website enquiry form to provide a brief description of the matter.

08

Does contacting the practice mean my matter has been accepted?

No. An initial enquiry helps us understand the nature of the matter. A professional engagement begins only after appropriate terms have been agreed.
